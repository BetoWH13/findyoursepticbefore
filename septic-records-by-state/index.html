Septic Records Search

Looking for the location of a septic tank or drain field? The original installation permit or septic system record is often the fastest way to find it.

Many counties maintain septic permits, wastewater system drawings, and inspection records through environmental health departments. Use the guide below to find the agency responsible for septic system records in your state.

State Agencies

Programs that regulate septic systems

Most septic permits are issued through environmental health or wastewater management programs. The agencies below commonly manage septic system approvals and records.

Maine

Subsurface wastewater permits and septic installation records are typically maintained by municipalities and plumbing inspectors.

Maine Subsurface Wastewater Program

New Hampshire

Septic system permits are managed through the Department of Environmental Services wastewater program.

New Hampshire Septic Systems

Vermont

Wastewater system and potable water permits include septic system installations and site plans.

Vermont Wastewater Permit System

Massachusetts

Septic systems are regulated under Title 5 and records are often maintained by local boards of health.

Massachusetts Title 5 Program

Tennessee

Tennessee maintains a searchable statewide septic permit database.

Tennessee Septic Records Search

Florida

Onsite sewage treatment systems are regulated through the Florida Department of Health.

Florida Onsite Sewage Program

Michigan

County environmental health departments regulate septic systems and maintain local records.

Michigan Septic Systems

Wisconsin

Private onsite wastewater treatment systems (POWTS) are regulated statewide.

Wisconsin POWTS Program

Ohio

Household sewage treatment systems are regulated through county health departments.

Ohio Septic Systems

Washington

Many counties maintain searchable septic permit databases and wastewater system records.

Washington Septic Records

California

Onsite wastewater treatment systems (OWTS) programs regulate septic systems statewide.

California OWTS Program

Alaska

Wastewater system engineering approvals and documentation.

Alaska Wastewater Systems

Important Notice

This website does not host septic records

This website does not maintain septic permit databases or property wastewater records.

All links on this page direct visitors to external government agencies, environmental health departments, or public record systems that may maintain septic permits and wastewater system documentation.

Availability of septic records varies widely depending on the county, the age of the property, and whether records have been digitized.

This guide simply helps homeowners identify the agencies that may hold septic system permits, installation plans, or inspection records.
