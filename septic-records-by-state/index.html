Septic Records by State

If you need to find where a septic tank, drain field, or septic lines were installed, the fastest clue is often the original permit or installation drawing.

Most septic permits are stored by county environmental health departments or wastewater programs. This page links to the state programs and record systems where septic permits are commonly searched.
