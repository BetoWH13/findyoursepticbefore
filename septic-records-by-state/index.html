Septic Records by State

One of the fastest ways to locate a septic tank or drain field is to review the original installation permit or wastewater system records.

Most septic permits are issued and stored by **county environmental health departments** or **state wastewater programs**. This page links to the agencies and databases where those records are commonly searched.

Important disclaimer

This website **does not operate, maintain, or host septic system databases**.

All links on this page lead to **external government agencies, county health departments, or public record systems** that may maintain septic permits or wastewater documentation.

Availability of septic records varies widely depending on the state, county, and age of the property. Some jurisdictions maintain searchable online databases, while others store records in physical archives.

This guide provides **navigation assistance only** and is not responsible for the accuracy, availability, or completeness of external records.
